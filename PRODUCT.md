# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

School security operators use a dedicated kiosk or installed PWA in busy, public arrival areas. Authorized administrators configure terminals and provision or revoke devices from the main MMSC Administration experience.

## Product Purpose

Capture Student and Employee attendance quickly and reliably through MMSC-issued RFID or QR credentials, including during network and service outages, while keeping final validation, identity, attendance rules, authorization, and audit data authoritative in the shared MMSC platform.

## Brand Personality

Calm, trustworthy, and immediate. The terminal should feel unmistakably MMSC, remain legible at a glance, and communicate successful capture without slowing the line.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble the administrative dashboard, a generic analytics product, a developer console, or an alarming security screen during ordinary offline operation. Avoid decorative kiosk chrome, unnecessary setup stages, and technical error copy in the primary capture surface.

## Design Principles

- Make the next physical action obvious within two seconds.
- Persist first, then confirm capture.
- Treat connectivity as synchronization state, not device identity.
- Keep exceptional and destructive operations intentional and separate from daily scanning.
- Preserve MMSC brand consistency without importing the Administration application shell.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.1 AA contrast, keyboard access, visible focus, 44-pixel minimum touch targets, reduced-motion preferences, zoom and reflow support, semantic live status announcements, and status communication that does not rely on color alone.
